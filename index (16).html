How the ticket works

Every order becomes a live kitchen ticket. As it moves through the kitchen and onto a rider's bike, it gets stamped — no refreshing, no calling the stall.

01

Placed

Your order and payment reach the stall the moment you check out.

02

03

Out for delivery

A nearby rider picks it up hot and heads your way.

04

Delivered

Ticket gets its final stamp the second it's in your hands.
